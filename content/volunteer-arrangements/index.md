Thanks for putting your hand up. Volunteers make WREC'kd events happen, and we're really grateful for your time. You don't need to be a member to help out.

### What happens next

- **Join the volunteer WhatsApp chat.** You'll get the link when you sign up, or the Race Director will send it to you. That's where we share updates, plans and the odd weather panic.
- **Marshal briefing.** We'll organise a briefing before the event so you know where you'll be, what to look out for and who to call.
- **The Race Director will be in touch** with your role, timings and anything else you need to know.

### Be ready for the backcountry

Our events run in remote New Zealand backcountry, and marshal spots can be exposed and quiet for long stretches. Bring warm and waterproof layers, food, water and anything else you'd want for a long day out in changeable weather. If you've got first aid training, a PLB or a radio, let us know: it all helps.

Look after yourself and each other, know your limits, and treat everyone at the event with respect. We want WREC'kd to be safe, welcoming and inclusive for everyone.

### The declaration

By signing up as a volunteer, you confirm that:

- you have disclosed any medical conditions or medications relevant to your participation, either on the registration form or in prior correspondence with the Race Director
- you have kept your emergency contact informed of your participation in this activity
- you are aware of the risks associated with the environment the event takes place in, including those stated on the RAMS
- you are volunteering of your own free will.
