## Just done: [Aorangi Undulator →](/undulator/#results)

**Saturday 31 October 2026 · Not for the weak!**

54 runners took on ~27 km and 2,000 m of relentless undulations from Mangatoetoe to the Putangirua Pinnacles. Luke Brown took the win in 4:10:57 and Zoe MacClure was first woman in 5:48:11. Thanks to everyone who ran, marshalled, drove and cooked sausages.
