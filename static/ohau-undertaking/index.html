The ŌhauUndertaking

A summer special

A WREC'kd event

The Ōhau Undertaking is a summer circuit through the northern Tararua.

Head up the Ōhau River, where progress may be interrupted by the occasional dip in one of the many swimming holes along the way.

A self-organised affair, check in at the huts as you go, continuing from South Ōhau Hut up to Dora Saddle, across to Waiopehu Hut, before a fast descent back to the Poads Road carpark.

No formal start time, no finish-line formalities. Set off when you're ready, make your way around the circuit, and aim to be back in time for the after-match function.
