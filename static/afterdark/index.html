AfterDark

Lights Out

The clocks go back on Sunday 4 April 2027. The evenings close in early, and WREC'kd heads out anyway.

The After Dark Series is WREC'kd's opening series: three-night runs in the hills about Te Whanganui-a-Tara to mark the end of daylight saving.

A WREC'kd series, proudly put on by the Wilderness Running and Endurance Collective Inc for our members.

The Series | Three Rounds
#1[April 4th]

Kapakapanui

Tararua Forest Park · Ngatiawa Road, Reikorangi

[11km] / 1,050 △ · Start 5pm

Nine unbridged river crossings in the first twenty minutes, then a steep, rooty climb through the bush to the 1,102 m summit. Kāpiti Island is out there somewhere in the dark.
#2[tbc]

East Whakanui

Remutaka Forest Park · Hine Road Recreation Reserve, Wainuiomata

19km / 1400m △ · Start 5pm

Straight up out of Wainuiomata in Remutaka and big trees. Steep, advanced track, and hard to follow in places even in daylight.
#3[tbc]

Dobson Loop

Tararua Forest Park · Kiwi Ranch Road, Kaitoke

· Start 5pm

Up to Puffer Saddle, a sharp drop to Smith Creek, a technical valley floor with small creek crossings, then the long climb back over the saddle. The clay is slippery at any time of year.
Carry
  • A headtorch that will last the whole round, plus a spare light or spare batteries.
  • An extra warm layer. It gets cold fast once the sun goes.
  • Charged phone and the rest of the essential gear.
