## Travel, Parking & Logistics

> **Note:** It's a 21 km drive between the finish line (Putangirua Pinnacles) and the start line (Mangatoetoe). Allow plenty of travel time. Accommodation is available at Te Kopi Homestead, Waimeha Campground or the finish DOC Camp at the Pinnacles.

Ample parking is available at the finish, however limited at the start. Please consider travel arrangements prior. 
[Trave list](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12KNWGYBgBs8udbeDhasYbFE3qLtlJYSwa0J69Pw67IY/edit?usp=sharing)

To help with congestion, please carpool where you can — team up with other [runners](/undulator) before race day. If you'd like to meet other entrants, consider arriving at registration earlier.

Warm clothing and other belongings can be left with volunteers at the start to transport to the finish.

## What to Bring

The Aorangi Undulator is a backcountry event and conditions can change quickly. Come prepared.

- Mandatory gear (minimum requirements): see the [full list on our website](https://wreckd.org.nz/boring/)
- Wear your best trail shoes — expect wet, slippery, technical river travel and mud, scree and precipitous descents, and very limited course markings. This is a route, not a track.

> ⚠ **MANDATORY:** All entrants must carry or have access to a PLB or satellite tracking device on course. If you are in trouble, stay put and call emergency services for rescue [Hire outlets →](https://www.mountainsafety.org.nz/learn/skills/communications/plb-hire)

Racing as a team, or need to update your details? [Email us](mailto:office@runs.nz?subject=Undulator%20Race%20Updates)
