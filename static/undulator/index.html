Aorangi Undulator
Not for the Weak

The Aorangi Undulator is a point-to-point trail race through the heart of the Aorangi Forest Park — one of New Zealand's most remote and seldom-visited wilderness areas. Expect relentless undulations.

A WREC'kd event, proudly put on by the Wilderness Running and Endurance Collective Inc for our members.

October 31st 2026 A WREC'kd Event
The Course | ~27km / 2,000m △
#1 Starting at Ngā Rā o Kupe (Kupe's Sail). Pick some many paths up the river to find Mangatoetoe Hut, continuing upstream, the first undulation is brief, before a precipitous descent leads to the next catchment- Otakaha Stream. Picking up the river flats on the true right you'll meet Kawakawa Hut
#2 The next undulation is set from a cool knife edge between tributaries. The undulation is better described as an awkward off-camber sidle through the native Ongaonga and rocky shingle. Leading to the Pararaki Hut.
#3 A Big hill. Opening out through Beach Forest and plenty tree fall, before a steep and scary drop in full send to Washpool Hut
#4 Much like the last. If you can afford the time, then a brief dogleg at the top brings good views of the unnumbered undulations to come. These false undulations/peaks are brief before Gorse Alley and the track to finish, the course is bookended with further signficiant geological features at the Putangirua Pinnacles. Smell the sausages!
